Your files never leave your device

The PDF Editor and PDF Password Protector on this site run entirely in your web browser. When you open a PDF, it is read and processed locally on your computer. Your files are not uploaded, stored, transmitted, or seen by us or any third party. When you close the tab, the file is gone from memory.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect personal information directly. We do not run our own analytics that identify you. The only data collection on this site comes from the third-party services described below.

Advertising and cookies

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Third-party libraries

The tools load open-source libraries (PDF.js, pdf-lib, QPDF-WASM) from public content delivery networks (CDNs) so the tools can run in your browser. Your browser requests these files directly from the CDN; the requested file is code, not your document.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
