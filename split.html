Split PDF

Extract the pages you want from a PDF — type a page range like 1-3,5,8-10. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

    Use commas for individual pages and hyphens for ranges. Pages are extracted in the order you list them.

    🔒 100% private: files are processed locally with pdf-lib and never leave your device.

    How to split a PDF

    Add one PDF using the box above. Once it loads you'll see its page count. Type the pages you want in the box — for example 1-3,5,8-10 — then click Extract selected pages to download a new PDF containing only those pages, in the order you listed them.

    Is it private?

    Yes. The split happens entirely in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library. Your file is never uploaded to a server.

    What page ranges can I use?

    Combine single pages and ranges freely: 2 for one page, 1-3 for a range, or 1-3,5,8-10 for a mix. Duplicate pages are removed automatically and any page beyond the document's length is ignored.
