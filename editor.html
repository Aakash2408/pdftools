Free online PDF editor — edit text, keep the fonts

Drop a PDF here or click Open PDF above.
  • Reads and renders every page (PDF.js).
  • Extracts each text run with its font family, size, weight & style.
  • Click any text to edit it in place — double-click a blank area to add new text.
  • Download rebuilds the PDF with your edits in the matched font (pdf-lib).
  • Search, page thumbnails, undo/redo, zoom.

🔒 100% private: everything runs in your browser. Your file is never uploaded to any server.
