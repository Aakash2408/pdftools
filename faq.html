Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF editor really free?

Yes. Both the editor and the password protector are free to use with no account required. The site is supported by ads.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Both tools run entirely in your browser. Your PDF is read and processed on your own device and is never sent to a server. That is also why the tools keep working even if your connection drops after the page loads.

How do I edit text in a PDF?

Open the editor, drop in a PDF, then click any block of text to edit it in place. The editor detects each text run's font family, size, weight and style so your edit blends in. Click Download to rebuild the PDF with your changes.

Will the fonts still match after I edit?

The editor maps each embedded font to its closest standard PDF font class (Helvetica, Times, Courier and their bold/italic variants) and embeds that on export. For most documents the result matches closely; highly stylized or custom fonts are approximated.

Can I add new text, not just edit existing text?

Yes. Double-click a blank area of a page to add a new text box. Added text is written into the downloaded PDF as well.

How do I password-protect a PDF?

Open the protector, choose your PDF, type a password, and download the encrypted file. It uses AES-256 encryption. Anyone opening the file afterwards will be asked for the password.

Can you recover a PDF if I forget the password?

No. The protection is real encryption; there is no backdoor and we never see your file or password. Keep your password safe.

Does it work on mobile?

The tools work in modern mobile browsers, though editing is easier on a larger screen.

Which browsers are supported?

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. The password protector needs a secure (https) connection to load its encryption module, which this site provides.
